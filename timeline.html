Sporting CP

2002–2003

Cristiano Ronaldo arrived in Lisbon from Funchal as a teenager with a reputation for speed and a habit of demanding the ball. His first senior season at Sporting CP produced five goals in 31 appearances, the first senior goal came against Moreirense in October 2002, and the Supertaça Cândido de Oliveira closed out a single season that had already changed the market.

  • 14 Aug 2002 Senior debut for Sporting CP — aged 17 years and 190 days, Ronaldo comes on against Inter in the UEFA Champions League third qualifying round at the Estádio José Alvalade, the start of the most decorated career in modern football.Age 17y 190dOfficial Debut
  • 7 Oct 2002 First senior career goal vs Moreirense — seven weeks after his debut, Ronaldo opens his senior account with two goals against Moreirense in the Primeira Liga: the first of 981.Goal #1⚽First Senior Goal

Manchester United

2003–2009

United paid £12.24m in August 2003 and handed him the legendary No. 7 shirt. He scored in the FA Cup final at 19, won three Premier League titles, the Champions League and the FIFA Club World Cup, and in December 2008 was named the world's best player at 23 — on 77 of 96 ballots.

  • 12 Aug 2003 Signs for Manchester United and takes the No. 7 shirt — Ronaldo signs for £12.24m and inherits the legendary United number 7, the shirt he has worn for the rest of his career.£12.24m🔴Manchester United
  • 12 Jun 2004 First Portugal goal — header against Greece — a stoppage-time header against Greece in the Euro 2004 group stage opens his international account, a record he extends to 146 goals in 234 caps.First Portugal goal⚽International Goal
  • 2 Dec 2008 Ballon d'Or at 23, named on 77 of 96 ballots — the first Ballon d'Or ever won by a Portuguese player, in a season that also delivered the Champions League, the FIFA Club World Cup and eight goals in Euro 2008 qualifying.Ballon d'Or #1🌟1st Ballon d'Or

Real Madrid

2009–2018

Real Madrid paid a world-record €94m in June 2009 and 80,000 packed the Bernabéu for the presentation. He answered with a Copa del Rey final header in a Clásico in his first season, a La Liga title won with a record 100 points and 46 league goals, and 63 goals in the 2012 calendar year — with 450 goals in 438 appearances for the club in all.

  • 10 Jun 2009 Signs for Real Madrid for a world-record fee — Ronaldo joins for a then record €94m, and 80,000 fans pack the Bernabéu for the presentation. He stays on the Spanish stage until 2018.€94m⚪Real Madrid
  • 24 May 2014 La Décima — the last-gasp penalty in Lisbon — Ronaldo converts the extra-time penalty at the Estádio da Luz to win a 4–1 Champions League final after extra time, giving Real Madrid a tenth European Cup and a personal 17-goal Champions League season.La Décima🏆10th European Cup
  • 30 Sep 2015 500th career goal and Champions League top scorer — his 500th goal arrives against Malmö in the season he passes Messi to become the all-time Champions League top scorer, a record he still holds.Goal #500⚡500 Goals
  • 18 Apr 2017 Fifth Ballon d'Or and a 100th Champions League goal — a hat-trick against Bayern Munich takes him past 100 Champions League goals; in December he collects his fifth Ballon d'Or in Paris.Ballon d'Or #5🌟5th Ballon d'Or

The Ballon d'Or years

2008–2017

Five Ballons d'Or: 2008, 2013, 2014, 2016 and 2017. Between them sit a 69-goal calendar year, the European Championship won as captain in Paris, four consecutive Champions League titles and the 500th career goal — the record that took him past every scorer in the competition's history.

  • 6 Sep 2013 Three goals in 15 minutes for Portugal — against Northern Ireland in a World Cup 2014 qualifier, Ronaldo scores three times inside 15 minutes, the fastest international hat-trick of his career, in the season he sets his 69-goal calendar-year record.Hat-trick in 15 minutes⚡Record Hat-trick
  • 10 Jul 2016 EURO 2016 champion as Portugal's captain — Ronaldo lifts the European Championship in Paris, finishing as the tournament's second-highest scorer with three goals and taking the Silver Boot.Euro 2016🏆European Champion

Juventus

2018–2022

He joined Juventus at 33 for €100m, scored a hat-trick in his first Champions League game there and took two Serie A titles in three seasons. The Nations League title came in June 2019 and the 700th goal of his career followed against Ukraine in October; in February 2020 he played his 1,000th senior appearance against SPAL.

  • 10 Jul 2018 Signs for Juventus at 33 — Ronaldo joins for €100m and wins two Serie A titles in three seasons, becoming the first player to win the top-scorer award in three different leagues.€100m🐂Juventus
  • 14 Oct 2019 The 700th goal of his career — Ronaldo reaches 700 senior goals against Ukraine, having already topped the Nations League scoring charts at the Finals.Goal #700⚽700 Goals
  • 22 Feb 2020 1,000th senior appearance — Ronaldo plays his 1,000th senior match for Juventus against SPAL, and later in the year scores his 100th and 101st goals for Portugal in a single match against Sweden.1,000th senior appearance📜1,000th Appearance

The Manchester United return

2021–2022

The return to Old Trafford in September 2021 brought back the No. 7 shirt and 24 goals in 38 appearances in his first season back. On the international stage he passed Ali Daei to become the outright leading scorer in men's international football, with a tenth international hat-trick — a world record — and in 2022 a hat-trick against Tottenham took him past Josef Bican.

  • 11 Sep 2021 The return to Manchester United — Ronaldo returns to Old Trafford on a two-year deal, scoring twice on his debut against Newcastle and adding 24 goals in all competitions that season.2nd spell🔴Man United Return

Al Nassr

2023–present

Al Nassr made him captain in January 2023, and with his 197th cap he became the most-capped player in men's international football — a record he has since carried to 234 caps. The marks kept coming: the 900th career goal in the Nations League in 2024, the 800th club goal and the 100th for Al Nassr in 2025 — the first player to score 100 goals for four clubs — and in 2026 the Saudi Pro League title, the 500th goal scored after turning 30 and a sixth FIFA World Cup.

  • 5 Sep 2024 The 900th goal of his career — a close-range finish against Croatia in the UEFA Nations League takes Ronaldo past 900 senior goals, the first player to score 40 or more goals in 14 different calendar years.Goal #900⚽900 Goals
  • 26 May 2025 800th club goal for Al Nassr — Ronaldo scores his 800th goal for a club against Al Fateh; two months later he becomes the first player to reach 100 goals for four different clubs, aged 40.800th club goal⚡800 Club Goals
  • 12 Oct 2026 Goal #983 — the last of a record career — Ronaldo's 983rd and most recent senior goal arrives for Al Nassr in the AFC Champions League Elite against Neftchi Fergana, closing a career of 983 goals in 1,342 appearances.Goal #983⚽983 career goals

Frequently Asked Questions: Ronaldo's Career Milestones

When did Ronaldo make his senior debut?▾

For Sporting CP on 14 August 2002 against Inter in the Champions League third qualifying round, aged 17; his first senior goals came seven weeks later against Moreirense.

When did Ronaldo score his first goal?▾

His first Portugal goal was a header against Greece at Euro 2004; his first Manchester United goal that same year was a free kick against Portsmouth.

When did Ronaldo win the Champions League?▾

First in 2008 with Manchester United, then four times in a row with Real Madrid in 2014, 2016, 2017 and 2018.

When did Ronaldo move to Real Madrid?▾

In June 2009, for a then world-record fee — with 80,000 in the Bernabéu for the presentation.

When did Ronaldo join Al Nassr?▾

In January 2023, making his debut as captain against Al-Ettifaq and scoring twice in a 2–2 draw.

How I put these numbers together

I count one thing: official senior first-team matches for Real Madrid, Manchester United, Juventus, Al Nassr, Sporting CP and Portugal — every league game, domestic cup, continental night and international, and nothing else. No youth football, no reserves, no friendlies dressed up as history.

How these numbers are checked

Season and calendar-year goals are tallied from full match records and checked against club and competition records plus free match-data feeds after every game. Timeline entries are year- and date-granular milestones; era goal bars split calendar years into non-overlapping spans so they add back up to the career total.

This is a one-person site, kept by hand. Totals only ever change when I have a named source and have checked it twice — never by a script, never on a rumour. If you spot something wrong, that date stamp at the top tells you exactly when I last looked.

Penalties (184), free kicks (66), hat-tricks (66) and assists (261) are widely published compiled tallies, shown as provisional until a true match-by-match hand count is finished.

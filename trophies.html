Essential Trophy & Award Facts

How many trophies has Ronaldo won?▾

How many Ballons d'Or has Ronaldo won?▾

Five — 2008, 2013, 2014, 2016 and 2017, the first at 23 years old.

Has Ronaldo won a major international title?▾

Yes — UEFA Euro 2016 as captain, and the UEFA Nations League in 2019 and 2025.

How many Champions Leagues has Ronaldo won?▾

Five: 2008 with Manchester United, then 2014, 2016, 2017 and 2018 with Real Madrid.

Test Your Cristiano Ronaldo Trophy & Records IQ

Think you know everything about Ronaldo's 35 trophies, 5 Ballons d'Or and world records? Answer 5 trivia questions below:

Which years did Ronaldo win the Ballon d'Or?▾

Five times: 2008, 2013, 2014, 2016 and 2017. The first came at 23, with the vote on 77 of 96 ballots.

How many Champions League titles has Ronaldo won?▾

Five — the first in 2008 with Manchester United, then 2014, 2016, 2017 and 2018 with Real Madrid. He is also the competition's all-time top scorer with 140 goals.

When did Ronaldo score his 500th career goal?▾

On 30 Sep 2015, against Malmö, in the season he passed Messi to become the all-time Champions League top scorer.

When did Ronaldo score his 900th career goal?▾

On 5 September 2024, a converted penalty against Croatia in the UEFA Nations League — the first player to reach 900 senior goals.

When did Ronaldo make his 1,000th senior appearance?▾

On 22 February 2020, for Juventus against SPAL.

Frequently Asked Questions: Cristiano Ronaldo Trophies

What is Ronaldo's most important trophy?▾

The 2016 European Championship — the title he captained Portugal to in Paris, as the tournament's second-highest scorer.

How many league titles has Ronaldo won?▾

Eight — 3 Premier League, 2 La Liga, 2 Serie A and 1 Saudi Pro League, as itemised in this archive's honours list.

How many individual awards has Ronaldo won?▾

Five Ballons d'Or, four European Golden Shoes, three UEFA Men's Player of the Year awards, the FIFA World Player of the Year in 2008, the FIFA Puskás Award in 2009, the Euro 2020 Golden Boot and The Best FIFA Men's Player in 2016 and 2017.

Is Ronaldo the most decorated player ever?▾

On team titles alone Messi leads with 46 to Ronaldo's 35. Ronaldo leads the scoring records — 981 goals, 140 of them in the Champions League, and 146 goals in 234 caps for Portugal.

How I put these numbers together

I count one thing: official senior first-team matches for Real Madrid, Manchester United, Juventus, Al Nassr, Sporting CP and Portugal — every league game, domestic cup, continental night and international, and nothing else. No youth football, no reserves, no friendlies dressed up as history.

How these numbers are checked

Season and calendar-year goals are tallied from full match records and checked against club and competition records plus free match-data feeds after every game. The trophy list is itemised from the published honours record: 32 club titles and 3 with Portugal, 35 in all.

This is a one-person site, kept by hand. Totals only ever change when I have a named source and have checked it twice — never by a script, never on a rumour. If you spot something wrong, that date stamp at the top tells you exactly when I last looked.

Penalties (184), free kicks (66), hat-tricks (66) and assists (261) are widely published compiled tallies, shown as provisional until a true match-by-match hand count is finished.

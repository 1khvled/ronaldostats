What Was Ronaldo's Greatest Year? The 69-Goal Career Record (2013)

See those goals by competition

2013 is Ronaldo's highest-scoring calendar year: 69 goals — 59 for Real Madrid and 10 for Portugal. The 59 club goals are the most he has scored for his clubs in a calendar year, and the year included his first international hat-trick: three goals in fifteen minutes against Northern Ireland.

The years behind it — 2012 (63), 2014 (61) and 2011 (60) — show how far ahead of his own normal 2013 was. See how those goals break down by competition.

How I put these numbers together

I count one thing: official senior first-team matches for Sporting CP, Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus, Al Nassr and Portugal — every league game, domestic cup, continental night and international, and nothing else. No youth football, no reserves, no friendlies dressed up as history.

How these numbers are checked

Season and calendar-year goals are tallied from full match records and checked against club and competition records after every game. The free-kick, penalty and hat-trick splits are a widely published tally, marked provisional until the match-by-match hand count is finished.

This is a one-person site, kept by hand. Totals only ever change when there is a named source and it has been checked twice — never by a script, never on a rumour. If you spot something wrong, the date stamp at the top tells you exactly when it was last checked.

Penalties (184), free kicks (66), hat-tricks (66) and assists (261) are widely published compiled tallies, shown as provisional until a true match-by-match hand count is finished.

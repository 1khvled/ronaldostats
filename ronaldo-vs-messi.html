Who has better all-time career statistics: Ronaldo or Messi?

Across all official senior matches, Cristiano Ronaldo has scored 981 goals and provided 261 assists in 1,340 appearances (0.93 goal contributions per match), while Lionel Messi has scored 936 goals and provided 408 assists in 1,181 appearances (1.14 contributions per match). Ronaldo leads the all-time goal tally (981 vs 936), the appearance count (1,340 vs 1,181), penalties (184 vs 109), hat-tricks (66 vs 62) and Champions League goals (140 vs 129). Messi leads in assists (408 vs 261), senior team trophies (46 vs 35), Ballons d'Or (8 vs 5), goals per match (0.79 vs 0.79) and direct free kicks (67 vs 66). Every row is a published total; the ranking is left to the reader.

Frequently Asked Questions: Ronaldo vs Messi

Who has more total career goals: Ronaldo or Messi? ▾

Cristiano Ronaldo has scored 981 senior goals in 1,340 appearances, a rate of 0.73 per match. Lionel Messi has 936 goals in 1,181 appearances, a rate of 0.79 per match. Ronaldo leads the total by 45 goals and has played 159 more matches; Messi leads the ratio by 0.06.

Who has more non-penalty goals: Ronaldo or Messi? ▾

Cristiano Ronaldo has scored 797 non-penalty goals. Messi's 936 goals include 109 penalties, which leaves 797 non-penalty goals. Messi leads on this measure, and because the set-piece splits behind both figures are published tallies rather than hand counts, the margin is given as two totals rather than as a difference.

Who has scored more free kicks: Ronaldo or Messi? ▾

Lionel Messi has scored 67 direct free-kick goals; Cristiano Ronaldo has 66. Messi leads by one. Ronaldo's free kicks came for Real Madrid, Manchester United, Juventus, Al Nassr and Portugal, and the set-piece tally is a widely published one that stays provisional until the match-by-match hand count is finished.

Who has more career assists: Ronaldo or Messi? ▾

Cristiano Ronaldo has 261 career assists, or 291 on Transfermarkt's wider criteria. Lionel Messi has 408, or 450 on the same criteria. Messi leads by 147 on the published senior tally.

Who has scored more penalties, and what are their conversion rates? ▾

Cristiano Ronaldo has scored 184 penalties from 220 attempts, an 83.6% conversion rate. Lionel Messi has scored 109 from 149 attempts, a 73.2% rate. Ronaldo leads by 75 penalties.

Who has more career hat-tricks: Ronaldo or Messi? ▾

Cristiano Ronaldo has scored 66 career hat-tricks, ten of them for Portugal, which is a world record. Lionel Messi has scored 62. Ronaldo leads by four.

Who performed better at the FIFA World Cup: Ronaldo or Messi? ▾

Lionel Messi has 21 World Cup goals in 26 matches, four of them in the knockout rounds. Cristiano Ronaldo has 11 goals across six World Cups, from 2006 to 2026, and in 2026 he became the first man to score at six of them. Messi leads the goal count by 10.

Who won more of the direct head-to-head meetings between Ronaldo and Messi? ▾

In 36 club meetings, Messi's sides won 16, Ronaldo's won 11, and 9 were drawn. The archive records the results and leaves the verdict to the reader.

Who has won more trophies and Ballons d'Or? ▾

Lionel Messi has 46 senior team trophies to Cristiano Ronaldo's 35. Ronaldo's count is the itemised one, 32 club trophies and 3 with Portugal, because the popular counts of 36 and 37 over-count the UEFA Super Cup and the domestic supercups. Messi also leads the Ballons d'Or, 8 to 5.

Why does the statistical argument split between Ronaldo and Messi? ▾

Because the two careers distribute their output differently. Ronaldo leads on total goals (981 to 936), penalties (184 to 109), hat-tricks (66 to 62) and Champions League goals (140 to 129). Messi leads on assists (408 to 261), senior team trophies (46 to 35), Ballons d'Or (8 to 5), goals per match (0.73 to 0.79) and free kicks (67 to 66). Every row of the tables above is a fact; the conclusion belongs to the reader.

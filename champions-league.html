Frequently Asked Questions: Champions League Record

How many Champions League goals did Ronaldo score for Real Madrid?

Cristiano Ronaldo scored 105 Champions League goals for Real Madrid between 2009 and 2018, the highest total for a single club in the competition's history. He added 21 for Manchester United and 14 for Juventus, 140 in all.

In which Champions League finals did Ronaldo score?

Ronaldo scored in five Champions League finals: 2008 for Manchester United against Chelsea, and 2014, 2016, 2017 and 2018 for Real Madrid against Atlético Madrid, Juventus and Liverpool. He won the competition five times, the first player to do so.

What is Ronaldo's highest scoring game in the Champions League?

On 7 March 2012, Ronaldo scored 5 goals in a single Champions League match against Bayer Leverkusen in a 7–1 win, the only five-goal haul in the modern Champions League.

How many Champions League assists has Ronaldo recorded?

Cristiano Ronaldo recorded 42 assists in the UEFA Champions League, also an all-time record in the competition.

How many trophies has Cristiano Ronaldo won?▾

35 senior team titles — 32 with clubs and 3 with Portugal. Popular counts of 36 or 37 over-count the UEFA Super Cup and the domestic supercups; 35 is the itemised figure. Individual honours include 5 Ballons d'Or (2008, 2013, 2014, 2016, 2017) and 4 European Golden Shoes. See the full trophy list.

What are Ronaldo's UEFA Champions League statistics?▾

Ronaldo has scored 140 goals in 183 Champions League matches and added 42 assists — all-time records in the competition. He won the tournament 5 times: with Manchester United in 2008 and with Real Madrid in 2014, 2016, 2017 and 2018. View the full data on our Champions League hub.

What are Ronaldo's Portugal records?▾

Ronaldo holds both all-time international records with 146 goals in 234 caps. He has scored 10 international hat-tricks, a world record, has captained Portugal since 2007, and has played at six FIFA World Cups (2006, 2010, 2014, 2018, 2022, 2026). Portugal won EURO 2016 and the Nations League in 2019 and 2025.

How far is Cristiano Ronaldo from 1,000 career goals?▾

With 981 goals, Ronaldo is 19 short of 1,000 senior career goals. No date is promised: he is 19 goals short, and the tracker updates after every match.

Which records and milestones has Ronaldo set?▾

His 900th career goal came on 5 September 2024, the first player to reach 900. The 500th followed on 30 September 2015, the 700th on 14 October 2019 and the 800th club goal on 26 May 2025; his 1,000th senior appearance was on 22 February 2020, his 100th Al Nassr goal on 23 August 2025 — making him the first player to reach 100 goals for four clubs — and his 234th Portugal cap on 27 September 2026. His best club season was 2011/12: 60 goals for Real Madrid.

🏟️ Club by Club & International Splits

  • Sporting CP (2002–2003): 5 goals in 31 official appearances. Debuted as a senior on 14 August 2002, aged 17.
  • Manchester United (2003–2009, 2021–2022): 145 goals in 346 appearances (0.42 per game). Won the Champions League and the FIFA Club World Cup in 2008, and the Premier League three times.
  • Real Madrid (2009–2018): 450 goals in 438 appearances (1.03 per game) — the most goals of any player for a single club in La Liga history. Won four Champions Leagues and three Pichichis.
  • Juventus (2018–2022): 101 goals in 134 appearances (0.75 per game). Serie A capocannoniere in 2021.
  • Al Nassr (2023–present): 134 goals in 157 appearances. Saudi Pro League Golden Boot in 2024 and 2025, and a record 35 league goals in the 2023/24 season.
  • Portugal (2003–present): 146 goals in 234 caps — both all-time international records. Won EURO 2016 and the Nations League in 2019 and 2025.

🏆 Major Competitions & Tournament Records

  • UEFA Champions League: 140 goals in 183 appearances and 42 assists — all-time records in the competition. Five titles: 2008, 2014, 2016, 2017 and 2018.
  • La Liga: 311 goals for Real Madrid, the competition's all-time record.
  • Saudi Pro League: 106 goals for Al Nassr, including a record 35 in the 2023/24 season.
  • Premier League: 103 goals for Manchester United. Serie A: 81 goals for Juventus. Primeira Liga: 3 goals for Sporting CP.
  • FIFA World Cup: six tournaments (2006, 2010, 2014, 2018, 2022, 2026) and, in 2026, the first man to score at six World Cups.
  • International hat-tricks: 10 for Portugal — a world record — plus the outright international goalscoring record he took from Ali Daei on 1 September 2021.

Real Madrid, 2009–2018 His Nine Years at the Bernabéu

Ronaldo at Real Madrid

Cristiano Ronaldo played for Real Madrid from 2009 to 2018: 438 appearances and 450 goals, 311 of them in La Liga and four Champions League titles. Against Barcelona he scored 18 El Clásico goals. Lionel Messi, on the other side of that fixture for almost all of it, has 26 El Clásico goals in 45 matches.

Landmarks of the Real Madrid Years

2011 · Copa del Rey Final

First Spanish Trophy

The stoppage-time header against Barcelona in the Copa del Rey final, his first trophy in Spain. A year later he reached 100 La Liga goals for the club.

Frequently Asked Questions: Ronaldo at Real Madrid

How many goals did Cristiano Ronaldo score against Barcelona? ▾

Ronaldo scored 18 El Clásico goals for Real Madrid between 2009 and 2018. The archive does not carry a verified match-by-match split of those goals by competition, nor a Clásico appearance count, so it publishes the goal total rather than an estimated split.

How many years did Ronaldo play for Real Madrid? ▾

Nine years, from 2009 to 2018: 438 appearances and 450 goals, 311 of them in La Liga, and four Champions League titles, the last of them won in 2018.

How many goals did Ronaldo score in each competition for Real Madrid? ▾

311 in La Liga, 105 in the Champions League, 22 in the Copa del Rey and 12 in the Supercopa de España, the UEFA Super Cup and the FIFA Club World Cup: 450 goals in 438 appearances.

How many hat-tricks did Ronaldo score? ▾

Ronaldo has scored 66 career hat-tricks, ten of them for Portugal, which is a world record. One of them came against Barcelona at the Santiago Bernabéu in 2014.

How does Ronaldo's El Clásico record compare with Messi's? ▾

Ronaldo's archive records 18 El Clásico goals against Barcelona for Real Madrid. Messi's published record is 26 El Clásico goals in 45 matches, almost all of them for Barcelona.

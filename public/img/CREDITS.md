## Club crests

The club and country badges in `public/img/teams/` are the real crests, not drawings:
Real Madrid, Manchester United, Juventus, Sporting CP, Al Nassr, Portugal and
FC Barcelona come from the public crest service at
https://crests.football-data.org/ (the club and country badges it publishes),
and the two it does not carry — Sporting CP and Al Nassr — come from the
club's own crest file on Wikipedia (Special:FilePath). Club crests are
trademarks of their owners; they are used here to identify the teams in
Ronaldo's career record, and every other image on this site is freely
licensed.
